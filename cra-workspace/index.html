Privacy policy

Panu Automation · Last updated: [DATE] · Terms of use

CRA Workspace runs entirely on Atlassian’s Forge platform, inside your Atlassian site. It sends no data to us or to anybody else. We cannot see what you enter into it.

Panu Automation (Panu Salo, sole trader), business ID [BUSINESS ID], [ADDRESS], Finland.
Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]

What the app stores, and where

In Forge storage, hosted by Atlassian for your site:

  • the product register: product names, versions, categories, dates;
  • for each requirement of each product: its state, a justification if you wrote one, links to evidence, and the Atlassian account id of the owner you chose and of the person who last changed it;
  • the component lists of the SBOMs you add (names, versions, package URLs, licences) and the account id of whoever added each;
  • which Jira work items are a product’s risks and reports (their ids, nothing else);
  • which deadline reminders have already been posted.

On your own Jira work items:

  • a risk’s rating, treatment and the account id of whoever signed it off, as an entity property;
  • a report’s kind and times, as an entity property;
  • comments recording each rating, sign-off, submission and reminder.

In your own Confluence, when you export: a page containing the report. Owners and signers appear in it as mentions.

The only personal data involved is Atlassian account ids, and through them the names Atlassian shows for those accounts. The app stores no names, email addresses or other attributes of its own.

What the app may do in your site

The permissions (scopes) the app asks your administrator to approve, and what each is used for:

ScopeUsed for
storage:appthe register, requirement states, SBOM component lists, links, reminder memory
read:jira-worklisting a product’s risks and reports; asking whether the user may edit a work item; listing projects
write:jira-workcreating a risk or report work item; setting its property; posting the trail and reminder comments
read:space:confluencelisting the spaces to export into
write:page:confluencecreating the exported page

The app acts as the user for everything a person does, so your Jira and Confluence permissions apply: a user sees only the work items they may browse and changes only those they may edit. It acts as the app in one place only: an hourly job reads report work items and posts a reminder comment. It changes no value.

What the app does not do

  • It makes no network request outside Atlassian. This is enforced by the platform: the app declares no external addresses and qualifies for Atlassian’s “Runs on Atlassian” programme.
  • It has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • It does not send your data to Panu Automation. We have no copy of it and no access to it.

As the vendor we receive from Atlassian what every Marketplace vendor receives: that a site installed, evaluated, bought or uninstalled the app, its licence details, and the technical and billing contact Atlassian holds for it. We use this to provide support and to manage licences. Atlassian’s own privacy policy governs how Atlassian handles it.

The app writes technical logs on Forge when something fails. They are visible to us as the developer. They contain error messages and Jira work item keys; they are written so as not to contain what you entered. [CHECK: the retention period Atlassian states for Forge logs, and say it here.]

Deleting your data

  • Removing a product removes its requirement states.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything in Forge storage for your site. This cannot be undone.
  • The entity properties and comments on your Jira work items, and exported Confluence pages, are yours and remain until you delete them in Jira and Confluence.
